Phone Memory & Storage Guarantee

How your orders stay safe when the app closes or phone restarts

✅

Does my data stay when I close the app or restart my phone?

YES! All your orders and settings are stored directly in your phone’s physical non-volatile hardware storage.

🔄

Closing the app or swiping it away

Your orders are not kept in temporary RAM. When you swipe the browser away or close the tab, everything remains saved on your phone’s drive.

🛡️

Dual-Storage Engine (LocalStorage + IndexedDB)

Every order is mirrored to both browser LocalStorage AND the phone IndexedDB database for automatic self-healing.
